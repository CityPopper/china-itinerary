Morning

  • MOCAPE in Futian — contemporary art/urban-planning museum and striking architecture.

Afternoon

  • Metro west to Shenzhen Bay for a waterfront walk.
  • Continue to Nantou Ancient City for old lanes, cafés, and creative shops.

Evening

  • Zhongshuge Bookstore at Happy Harbor — see the famous spiral bookshelf from your reel.
  • Dinner around Happy Harbor, then return to Futian.

Transportation

Futian hotel → MOCAPE · Metro~10–20 minChildren’s Palace area; easy first stop from a Futian base.
MOCAPE → Shenzhen Bay / Nantou · Metro~30–45 minFastest practical cross-city option without a car.
Nantou → Zhongshuge (Happy Harbor) · Metro / DiDi~25–40 minZhongshuge is near Linhai Station on Line 5; DiDi is convenient if you are tired.
