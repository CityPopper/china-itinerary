Morning

  • Huaqiangbei electronics district — gadgets, accessories, components, and tech malls.

Afternoon

  • BitCity 次元小镇 at HUA Plaza — make this the main anime/ACG shopping stop.
  • Then head to Dongmen; add NWH Dimension 9 if you still want more anime merchandise, blind boxes, claw machines, and themed shopping.

Evening

  • Dinner around Dongmen or return to Futian.
  • Optional foot massage or spa after the shopping marathon.

Transportation

Hotel → Huaqiangbei · Metro~15–25 minBest for avoiding traffic.
Huaqiangbei → BitCity → Dongmen · Metro~10–30 min per legAll three are straightforward by metro; use DiDi if you accumulate too many bags.

Planning note

Keep passport/payment apps handy; many stores are easiest with Alipay/WeChat Pay.
