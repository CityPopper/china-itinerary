Morning

  • High-speed rail Shenzhen North → Guangzhou South.
  • Start with Tianhe / Taikoo Hui / Grandview Mall for polished shopping.

Afternoon

  • Beijing Road for a more local pedestrian-shopping atmosphere.
  • Optional Baima clothing market if wholesale-fashion browsing interests you.

Evening

  • Cantonese dinner.
  • Return Guangzhou South → Shenzhen North by high-speed rail.

Transportation

Shenzhen North → Guangzhou South · High-speed rail~30–45 minFastest non-air route; frequent departures.
Guangzhou South → Tianhe / Beijing Road · Metro~35–55 minGuangzhou is large—allow time for cross-city movement.
Guangzhou South → Shenzhen North · High-speed rail~30–45 minBook a return with enough dinner buffer.

Planning note

This works best as a shopping-first day; trying to combine every major landmark will make it feel rushed.
