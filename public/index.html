Set up Alipay before travelling

  1. Install Alipay, register with your Canadian mobile number and set the language to English.
  2. Add an international payment card and complete any requested passport verification before leaving. Have a Visa or Mastercard available as a backup to Amex.
  3. Find DiDi inside Alipay for ride-hailing. Explore the Transport section for city transit options when you arrive.
  4. Keep access to verification texts on your registered number and arrange mobile data for China.

Use Alipay as your main payment app for the trip. Keep a payment card and some RMB cash available as backups.

Confirm late arrival with the hotel

Call +86 755 8383 8888 before travelling. Ask the hotel to hold your room for arrival after midnight, and ask whether a paid airport pickup can be arranged, including the price and meeting point.

Book the night of November 7 even if you reach the hotel on November 8 after midnight. Your booking must cover that overnight stay.

“My flight CA 1307 lands at Shenzhen Airport at 23:30 on November 7. I may reach the hotel after midnight. Please note my late arrival and hold my room. Can you quote an airport pickup and confirm where the driver would meet me?”

Airport → Sheraton Shenzhen Futian Hotel

For your 23:30 arrival, take a car directly to the hotel. Allow roughly 40–60 minutes for the drive after collecting your bags; this is a planning estimate.

Book DiDi through Alipay

  1. Collect your bags and connect to mobile data or airport Wi-Fi. Open Alipay and search for DiDi; complete any additional setup it requests.
  2. Set the pickup to your arrival terminal at Shenzhen Baoan International Airport. Enter Sheraton Shenzhen Futian Hotel as the destination and check it matches the address in the Hotel tab.
  3. Choose a ride type, review the estimated fare and selected payment method, then request the car when you are ready to reach the pickup area.
  4. Follow the app’s assigned pickup area, gate and meeting-point instructions. Ride-hailing pickup is separate from the taxi queue.
  5. Match the car’s number plate to your booking before boarding. Use in-app messages if you need to coordinate with the driver.

Ride-hailing operates 24 hours. If payment setup or finding your pickup is inconvenient, choose the Taxi instructions for the official airport queue.

Take an official airport taxi

  1. Collect your bags, then follow signs to the Ground Transportation Center (GTC).
  2. Go to the GTC’s second floor, east side, outside Gate 13 for the official taxi queue. Taxis operate 24 hours.
  3. Show the driver the hotel name and Chinese address in the Hotel tab. Take a metered taxi and keep the receipt.

Budget roughly RMB 150–200 for the late-night ride; this is a planning estimate. The fare depends on route, waiting time, tolls and surcharges. Keep RMB cash available as a payment backup.
