Morning

  • Train toward Yangshuo Station, then short transfer to Xingping Ancient Town.
  • Explore old lanes and riverfront before the busiest part of the day.

Afternoon

  • Lunch in Xingping.
  • Hanfu rental + hair/makeup.
  • Photo session around old streets, stone lanes, and Li River scenery.

Evening

  • 20 RMB banknote viewpoint / Li River around golden hour.
  • Dinner, then return to Guilin.

Transportation

Guilin → Yangshuo Station · High-speed rail~25–35 minYangshuo Station is near Xingping rather than central Yangshuo.
Yangshuo Station → Xingping · Taxi / DiDi~15–25 minSimplest local connection.
Xingping → Guilin · Taxi + HSR~1–1.5 hr totalReturn after dinner or sunset.

Planning note

Book hanfu styling once your rail timing is fixed; late afternoon gives the best light for river photos.
