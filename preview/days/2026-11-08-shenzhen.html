Morning

  • Arrive in Shenzhen and clear immigration/baggage.

Afternoon

  • Airport → Futian hotel by metro or DiDi.
  • Check in and orient yourself around the neighborhood.

Evening

  • Pick one easy first-night food option: Shuiwei 1368 for a casual food crawl, Riad by the Theatre for a photogenic dinner, or HeyTea at COCO Park for dessert and drinks.
  • Keep the evening close to the hotel if jet lag wins.

Transportation

Airport → Futian · Metro / DiDi~35–60 minFastest practical option depends on arrival airport/terminal and traffic.

Food logistics

Futian hotel → Shuiwei 1368 · Metro / walk~10–20 minBest flexible food-street option on night one.
Futian hotel → Riad by the Theatre · Metro / DiDi~10–20 minGood if you want one stylish sit-down spot.
Futian hotel → COCO Park / HeyTea · Walk / Metro~5–20 minEasy light option for dessert or a drink.

Planning note

Recommended hotel base: Futian, for strong metro access and convenient connections to Shenzhen North. Night one is intentionally flexible: choose the option that matches your energy after arrival.
